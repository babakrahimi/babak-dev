01

Approach

  • readable > clever

    Most code is read more than it is written. I optimise for the next person who has to understand it.

  • composable, documented, tested

    Reusable means easy to find, understand, test, and change. Documentation and tests are part of the component.

  • measured, not assumed

    Profile first. Performance discussions get much more useful once there is data.

  • mentorship over gatekeeping

    Knowing a codebase well should make it easier for other people to contribute to it.
